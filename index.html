    7 din ke FREE trial mein kya milta hai?

    Instant Broadcast portal (web + mobile app) ke saare features — broadcast, team inbox, chatbot flow builder aur lead integrations. AI Chatbot aur AI Calling bhi try kar sakte ho — popup mein tick karo. Setup mein hamari team call / screen-share pe help karti hai.

    Trial ke baad kya hoga?

    7 din mein khud dekh lo ki aapke business mein kaam kar raha hai ya nahi. Aage badhna ho to aapke business ke hisaab se plan hamari team batayegi. Pasand na aaye to kuch nahi karna.

    WhatsApp message ka charge alag kyun hai?

    WhatsApp (Meta) har business message pe charge leta hai. Instant Broadcast ka rate card: marketing ₹1.12, utility aur OTP ₹0.15 per message. Yeh charge WhatsApp (Meta) ka hota hai, trial mein bhi lagta hai.

    Kya mera abhi wala WhatsApp number chalega?

    Haan, zyada tar cases mein same number Official API pe le sakte hain. Setup call pe hamari team aapke number ke liye best tareeka batayegi.

    Setup kitne time mein hota hai? Kya developer chahiye?

    Developer ki zaroorat nahi. Hamari team call aur screen-share pe number connect karne, contacts import karne aur pehla broadcast bhejne mein help karti hai.

    AI Chatbot kaise seekhta hai?

    Aap apne products, rates, timing aur common sawaal dete ho; chatbot unhi ke basis pe jawab deta hai. Jo sawaal woh handle na kar sake, woh chat aapki team ko chali jaati hai.

    AI Calling agent kya karta hai?

    Nayi lead aate hi woh lead ko call karta hai, requirement poochta hai aur demo ya visit ka time book karta hai. Call ka summary aapki team ko milta hai.

    Mobile app hai?

    Haan — Android (Google Play) aur iPhone (App Store) dono pe. Web dashboard bhi saath mein.

    Bulk message bhejne se number ban toh nahi hoga?

    Official WhatsApp Business API, WhatsApp ka approved tareeka hai. Phir bhi WhatsApp ki policies follow karni hoti hain — sirf un customers ko marketing bhejo jinhone haan kaha ho, aur STOP ka option do. Setup pe hum yeh samjhate hain.
